Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-27

"Vinyl Picks Community Crate" (the "App") is a personal, non-commercial project run by an individual creator to automate a weekly Instagram content series. This policy explains what data the App processes and how.

What the App does

Each week, the App reads the public comments on the operator's own Instagram Reel posts, uses them to identify music (artist/album/song) recommendations, and compiles the most-recommended picks into a Spotify and Apple Music playlist. A summary is emailed to the operator.

What data is processed

  • Instagram comments: the public username, comment text, and timestamp of comments on the operator's own posts.
  • This data is used only to identify and rank music recommendations. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to compiling the weekly playlist.

Where data is stored and shared

  • Comment data is stored in a private, local database controlled by the operator. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except as needed to operate the App:
  • OpenAI — comment text is sent to OpenAI's API to extract structured music recommendations. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Spotify and Apple Music — used only to create/update the resulting playlists; no comment data is sent to these services. See Spotify's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

Comment data is retained to support the weekly playlist history. Anyone whose comment was processed by the App may request deletion of their data by contacting the operator at the address below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally as the App evolves. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
